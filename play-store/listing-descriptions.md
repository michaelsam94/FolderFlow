## Full description (4000 chars max)
FolderFlow helps you turn a crowded app drawer into clear, useful flows.

Create smart folders from categories, tags, recent installs, or idle apps. Build a Work flow, group apps with custom color-coded tags, review newly installed apps, and spot apps you have not opened in a while.

Key features:

- Smart folders based on rules
- Custom tags with palette colors
- App index with search, categories, and sorting
- Idle scanner for apps past your dormancy threshold
- Editable and removable custom folders
- Usage access support for better last-used detection

FolderFlow is built for quick cleanup and everyday organization. Use it to find what matters, keep similar apps together, and make old or rarely used apps easier to review.

FolderFlow works locally on your device. Usage access is optional and is used only to improve idle-app detection.
